    Getting around

    The verdict: no tourist pass — pay as you go

    No day pass or Lisboa Card pays for itself on this itinerary; the plan is walk-heavy, so pay-as-you-go wins by a wide margin.

    • Lisbon: buy a Navegante occasional card (€0.50) per person at the airport metro station and load ~€20 of zapping credit. Zapping fares: Metro/Carris €1.72 · CP Sintra trains €2.05 each way · Cacilhas ferry €1.60 · bus 3001 to Cristo Rei €0.85. Total for every planned ride: ~€18 per person (~€36 for two).
    • Porto: buy nothing. The days are on foot, and Campanhã ↔ São Bento is free with the long-distance train ticket (within 1 hour). Only the Matosinhos swap would need an Andante card (€4.30 per person all-in).
    • Sintra: Scotturb 24h hop-on ticket (buses 434 + 435) — €13.50, or €10.96 bought online.
    • Tram 15E takes no cash — zapping or contactless only. Pre-load rather than paying onboard cash fares.
    • Taxi/Bolt notes: Wed arrival Santa Apolónia → the flat ~€7–10 · Thu night — the Christmas spine is all walking, no taxi · Sat night — dinner is on your square · Sun airport run ~€12–16 (5:30 AM taxi — the first Sunday Metro is 6:30 AM, too late).
    • Key timings: last ferry back from Cacilhas 1:20 AM · last bus 3001 back from Cristo Rei 8:35 PM · Teleférico de Gaia December hours 10:00 AM–6:00 PM.
